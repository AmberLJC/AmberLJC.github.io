About Me

I'm building recursive self-improvement infrastructures — systems that get stronger the more they're used.

Before that, I got my PhD in Computer Science from University of Michigan (UMich), advised by Prof. Mosharaf Chowdhury. My research focused on building efficient systems that enable researchers to push the boundary of machine learning, including LLM serving systems, LLM pre-training systems (Meta Llama&Avocado), and Agentic RL. To contribute to the research community, I maintain comprehensive paper collections on Private ML Systems and LLM Systems.

I'm always eager to engage in meaningful discussions about research and explore potential academic collaborations. Feel free to reach out via email or schedule a meeting to connect!

What is AI for Science, an AI Co-Scientist, and AI-Native Research Infrastructure?

I'm building AI co-scientists for computational research. I co-lead Curie, the first AI-agent framework for rigorous automated scientific experimentation, and co-created EXP-Bench (ICLR 2026), the benchmark measuring whether agents can conduct AI research experiments end-to-end.

On the broader AI-native research ecosystem, I argue that the academic paper itself must evolve for an agent-driven world. In The Last Human-Written Paper: Agent-Native Research Artifacts, I propose ARA — a machine-executable research package that replaces narrative papers with structured layers (scientific logic, executable code, exploration graph, raw evidence), eliminating the storytelling and engineering taxes that block AI from reproducing and extending science.

Underneath, I build the systems layer that lets this ecosystem run at scale. My PhD thesis is on LLM infrastructure, spanning my expertise across LLM serving, pre-training, and post-training systems, and I was named a Machine Learning and Systems Rising Star (Class of 2023). Through intern, part-time, and full-time roles at Meta Superintelligence Lab, I worked on the pre-training and post-training infrastructure for LLMs, powering one of the largest LLM training stacks in the world. My broader work spans efficient LLM serving (Andes), Agentic RL systems, and energy-aware LLM serving (ML.ENERGY Benchmark, NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight).
